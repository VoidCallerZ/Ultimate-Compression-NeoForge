**Compress almost every vanilla block and item — 9 in, 1 out.**  
Available for **Fabric** • **NeoForge** • **Forge**

 

![](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/description/304462/description_33a081f1-084f-4049-a58e-391a2cf00435.png)

Tired of chest monsters and overflowing storage rooms? **Ultimate Compression** shrinks your stockpiles by letting you compress nearly all vanilla Minecraft blocks and items into dense, space-saving variants. Nine blocks go in, one compressed block comes out — and every recipe is fully reversible, so your resources are never locked away.

Compression isn't just for storage, either. Compressed ingots and ores can be forged into **upgraded tools and armor** with increased durability and special effects, all the way up to the mighty **Ultimate Compressed** tier.

 

![](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/description/304462/description_d26d789e-4146-4034-abfb-fd18fa4efd2e.png)

*   **Compressed blocks & items** — compact variants of nearly all vanilla blocks, including Grass Blocks, Coarse Dirt, Podzol, Dripstone, Ancient Debris, Sponges, Bricks, Purpur, Ice, Prismarine… and many more.
*   **Compressed gear** — tools and armor crafted from compressed ingots and ores, with boosted durability and unique effects.
*   **The Ultimate tier** — hunt down Ultimate Compressed Ore to craft the most powerful armor set and tools in the mod.
*   **Fully reversible** — decompress anything back into its original blocks whenever you need it.
*   **Storage-friendly by design** — perfect for survival worlds, skyblock grinds, and storage-hungry modpacks.

*   **Mod loaders:** Fabric and NeoForge on Minecraft 1.21+, with legacy Forge builds for 1.20.1 and below.
*   **JEI (Just Enough Items):** All compression recipes appear in JEI for easy lookup.
*   **Create:** All compressed block recipes can be packed using Create. The compressed logs can also be sawed into planks.
*   **Modpacks:** Ultimate Compression is MIT licensed — include it freely in any modpack.
